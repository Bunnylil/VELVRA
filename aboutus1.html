Our Story

VELVRA was founded with a simple mission: to provide premium athletic wear that combines style, performance, and sustainability. We believe in creating products that not only look good but also empower you to perform at your best.

Who We Are

VELVRA is more than just a fashion brand. We're a community of athletes, designers, and innovators who are passionate about creating premium athletic wear that helps you reach your full potential.

Founded in 2015, we've grown from a small startup to a global brand with a presence in over 30 countries. Our commitment to quality, innovation, and sustainability has earned us the trust of athletes and fashion enthusiasts worldwide.

We work with top-tier diverse brands worldwide to bring you the best in athletic fashion, from our iconic 90's Mixtape Collection to our functional Modern Utility Collection.

Our Core Values

Sustainability

We're committed to reducing our environmental footprint by using sustainable materials and ethical manufacturing processes.

Quality

We never compromise on quality. Every product is designed to last and perform under the most demanding conditions.

Community

We believe in building a community of like-minded individuals who share our passion for sports and fashion.

Our Journey

2015

VELVRA was founded with a small collection of athletic wear designed for performance and style.

2017

Launched our first flagship store and expanded our product line to include footwear.

2019

Introduced our sustainable collection made from recycled materials and eco-friendly fabrics.

2021

Expanded to international markets with stores in Europe and Asia.

2023

Launched our 90's Mixtape and Modern Utility Collections to critical acclaim.

Today

Continuing to innovate and expand our product offerings while staying true to our core values.
